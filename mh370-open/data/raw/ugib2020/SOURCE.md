## Key UGIB 2020 locations (footer page numbers)
- p.4–5: Fig. 3 and Fig. 4 route summaries, and §2.1 route overview.
- p.7: §3, satellite probability peak at 180°T and 34.3°S.
- p.18–19: §5.2.2–5.2.3, initial conditions and the hypothesised path along the FIR boundaries.
- p.24–25: §6.2, best-fit parameters for Trial 815.
- p.31: §6.4 and Fig. 24, FMT route map with legend waypoints.
- p.34: FMT design rationale.
- p.36: §6.5, MEFE at 00:17:30.
- p.41: §7.1, "simple" no-descent FMT alternative.
- p.44: Figs. 37 and 38, satellite-only probabilities.
- p.51–57: §8, LEP, navigation-error budget and search areas.
- p.108: Table C-1, legs with times, altitudes and speeds.
- p.117: §D.2, list of FMT manoeuvres.
- p.127–138: App. E, FMT route; Table E-1 on p.130 gives event times and positions.
- p.162–169: App. G, waypoint identification (Fig. G-5), bearing sensitivity (Fig. G-7), Table G-1 (Trial 854 residuals), and §G.9.
