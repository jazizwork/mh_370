Meanwhile, the ocean started giving pieces back. In July 2015, a wing part called a flaperon washed up on ray-union Island. Over the next two years, more pieces turned up in Mozambique, South Africa, Tanzania, Mauritius and Madagascar. In all, about twenty have been judged likely or certain to be from the plane. Nothing was found in Western Australia.

Oceanographers tried to run the currents backwards to find where the debris started. They didn't agree. Some studies landed near 35 degrees south. Others landed much further north, between 25 and 30. We'll come back to why.
