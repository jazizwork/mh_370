# Visuals for video/script.md

One line per still in `video/visuals/` (1920×1080 PNG): what it shows and where the numbers come from. Regenerate with `.venv/bin/python -I scripts/video_visuals.py`. All times on screen are Malaysian local time (UTC+8) unless marked UTC. Visuals marked ILLUSTRATION are schematic. On-screen source lines name public sources only; the file-level provenance is listed here.

- **V01_title** — Title card over a faint map of the 7th arc. Arc: src/mh370/geo.py; coastlines: Natural Earth.
- **V02_doppler_concept** — ILLUSTRATION: synthetic Doppler S-curve, f = f0/(1 - M cos theta) with retarded time; f0 = 100 Hz, v = 70 m/s, 500 m closest approach, c = 343 m/s (formula: reports/Aircraft Doppler audio geolocation.md).
- **V03_flight_path** — Map: KLIA -> IGARI (transponder) -> Penang -> VAMPI -> MEKAR -> last radar 02:22 MYT, dashed '?' arrow south. Waypoints and radar point from src/mh370/satcom_paths.py; KLIA/IGARI/Penang coordinates from the brief; route simplified to straight segments.
- **V04_arcs_map** — Map of the seven BTO rings (18:25, 19:41, 20:41, 21:41, 22:41, 00:10:58, 00:19:29 UTC = 02:25-08:19 MYT), south of 12N only, 7th arc emphasised, satellite sub-point at 00:19:29. Contours of geo.predicted_bto at sea level for the BTO values in geo.HANDSHAKES.
- **V05_bfo_chart** — Chart: measured BFO (8 values used in the fit from the public log; hollow = 18:25:27 log-on and 00:19:29 descent values from geo.HANDSHAKES, not used) vs the BFO predicted along the maximum-likelihood LNAV path (baseline SMC run), fitted bias included; residuals checked against the stored best-fit residuals. No northern-route curve (not computed credibly).
- **V06_search_timeline** — Timeline 2014-2027: surface/pinger search (8 Mar-28 Apr 2014), ATSB sonar search (Oct 2014-17 Jan 2017, ~120,000 km2), OI 2018 (22 Jan-29 May 2018, >112,000 km2), OI 2025-26 (25-28 Mar 2025 + 31 Dec 2025-23 Jan 2026, 7,571 km2 in 28 days), extension to 30 Jun 2027 with 7,428.54 km2 remaining. Sources: outputs/search/README.md, reports/MH370 open data search avenues.md, research_notes (official_investigation_and_search.md).
- **V07_searched_map** — Map of all reconstructed search polygons by campaign (outputs/search/search_polygons.geojson), 7th arc, and the OI contract's remaining NW strip ('OI2025_26_remaining_NW_strip_calibrated_7428'). Union = 255,609 km2 per outputs/search/README.md.
- **V08_debris_map** — Map of the 15 debris find sites (18 items; SIR confirmed/almost certain/highly likely, Jul 2015-Jan 2017) from outputs/drift/debris_items.csv, flaperon highlighted, 7th arc, 'none found in Western Australia'.
- **V09_leads_map** — Map of candidate points (Kadri crossing 25.73S 101.37E, Stead/LANL 40.97S 81.57E, Anderson 8.36S 107.92E, WSPR 29.128S 99.934E, UGIB 34.23S 93.78E, CSIRO 35.6S 92.8E), HA01 bearings 306.18 and 246.9 deg, HA01/IMOS recorder positions from src/mh370/acoustic.py, 7th arc.
- **V10_sound_footprint** — ILLUSTRATION: cross-section ray trace for a source at 10.7 km in a linear sound-speed profile (+28 C surface, -54 C aloft, no wind, from reports/MH370 acoustic tracking feasibility.md). Limiting depression angle 31 deg; audible strip +/-36 km (report: ~25-40 km each side, an inference).
- **V11_approach_diagram** — Flow diagram: satellite / drift / sound / searched seabed -> combined probability (incl. the broad 'model is wrong' mixture component, outputs/combine/README.md 'Model-misspecification mixture') -> ranked areas. Sound arrow dashed: the acoustic module found nothing above chance and no acoustic layer entered the combination (outputs/acoustic/README.md, outputs/combine/README.md).
- **V12_satcom_profile** — Chart: end-point latitude along the 7th arc (outputs/satcom/arc_profile_v2.csv; shown smoothed over 0.3 deg): recommended p_v2 (median 38.17S, 90% 36.46-39.51S, shaded; outputs/satcom/summary_v2.json), the descent-at-18:40 variant p_v2_sens_descent1840 (median 35.82S), the v1 layer p (dashed) and DSTG Fig 10.3 digitised (a 00:19:29 crossing pdf); 34.23S UGIB hotspot / 2025-26 search marked. outputs/satcom/README.md section 13.
- **V13_satcom_paths** — Map: best fit + 25 posterior paths for the LNAV and constant-true-track modes (baseline SMC samples re-run through PathModel with ERA5), the best fit of the top waypoint component FMT -> MUTMI -> constant true track (routes/baseline_WPT_MUTMI_CTT_n20000_s4*.npz re-run through satcom_routes.RouteModel), and UGIB's published route (scripts/satcom_ugib_replay.state_at). Median crossing and 90% range = p_v2 impact median 38.17S, 36.46-39.51S (outputs/satcom/summary_v2.json); FMT 18:32-18:40 UTC = 02:32-02:40 MYT; UGIB route fits only with its descent at the 18:40 call (level: -39 Hz, outputs/satcom/README.md section 13.5).
- **V14_drift_profiles** — Chart: four drift likelihood profiles along the arc, each exp(loglik - max): uncorrected (peak 29.4S), default speed-corrected (33.4S), flaperon-only generic (30.1S), flaperon with CSIRO leeway (38.1S). outputs/drift/arc_profile.csv, profile_summary.csv.
